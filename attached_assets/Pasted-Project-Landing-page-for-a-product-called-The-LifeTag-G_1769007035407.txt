Project: Landing page for a product called “The LifeTag”

Goal:
Create a simple, trustworthy landing page for Pakistan that explains and sells The LifeTag — a QR code sticker placed on motorcycle helmets that helps people access emergency information if the rider is injured.

Context

The LifeTag is a QR-based emergency identity system for bike riders in Pakistan.
In case of an accident, any bystander can scan the QR code on the helmet and instantly see:

Rider’s name

Blood group

Emergency contact numbers (tap to call)

Basic medical information (allergies / conditions)

No app. No login. No delay.

Target Audience

Motorcycle riders in Pakistan (18–40)

Daily commuters

Delivery riders

People who care about safety but don’t trust complex systems

Tone & Style

Trustworthy

Human

Simple

Safety-focused

Not “techy”

Mix of English + Roman Urdu

Emotion-driven, not feature-heavy

Landing Page Structure
1. Hero Section

Headline:
“Agar main behosh ho jaun, mera helmet bole.”

Subheadline:
The LifeTag is a QR code sticker for your helmet.
Scan it to instantly contact emergency contacts and see critical medical info.

Primary CTA button:
“Get The LifeTag”

Secondary CTA:
“See How It Works”

2. How It Works (3 steps)

Stick The LifeTag QR on your helmet

In case of accident, anyone scans it

Emergency contact & blood group appear instantly

Simple icons, minimal text.

3. Why The LifeTag Exists

Short paragraph explaining:

Accidents happen

Riders often unconscious

Phones are locked

People want to help but don’t know how

Include a strong line:

“Seconds matter. Information saves lives.”

4. What Information Is Shown

Bullet list:

Name

Blood group (highlighted)

Emergency contacts

Critical medical notes

Add reassurance:

Read-only

No CNIC

No address

No tracking

5. Why This Matters in Pakistan

Short section:

High bike usage

Weak emergency systems

People rely on bystanders

This helps ordinary people help faster

6. Call to Action

Headline:
“Safety is not optional.”

CTA button:
“Protect Yourself with The LifeTag”

Small text:
One-time setup. Lifetime peace of mind.

7. Footer

Brand name: The LifeTag

Short line:
A rider safety initiative

Simple contact placeholder

No social clutter
